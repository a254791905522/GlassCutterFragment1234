About the game

GlassCutterFragment is a fast paper-folding brain-teaser. Every level lays out overlapping colored flaps on a grid. Your job is to fold them into the center in the exact right order so the hidden pattern snaps into place — while a strict countdown keeps the pressure on. Miss the sequence or run out of seconds, and the fold fails. Nail it quickly to earn stars, coins, and a cleaner path through the level map.

How to play

  1. 1 Study which flaps overlap and which edge must fold first.
  2. 2 Tap colored flaps in the correct sequence toward the center.
  3. 3 Recreate the hidden pattern before the timer hits zero.
  4. 4 Grab a +15 sec boost if you need one more fold to win.
